Questions? We've Got Answers

How do I receive the coloring pages?

Right after checkout, you'll get an email with your download link. The pages come as a PDF that opens on any phone, tablet, or computer.

Is this a physical book?

No. It's a digital PDF you print at home, at school, or at any print shop. Nothing is shipped.

How many times can I print?

As many times as you like, for your family, your classroom, or your community program. Reselling or sharing the PDF file is not allowed.

Will it print on regular US Letter paper?

Yes. Just choose "Fit to page" in your print settings. Every page has wide margins, so nothing gets cut off.

What ages is it for?

The pages are designed for kids ages 3 to 10, with bold lines and large spaces. Older kids and adults enjoy them too.

Is the content respectful of Jewish tradition?

Yes. There are no depictions of God and no Divine Name in Hebrew, and characters wear modest clothing. The artwork is 100% original.

Can I use it in my school or synagogue?

Absolutely. Teachers and program leaders can print copies for their own students and events.

What if I don't like it?
